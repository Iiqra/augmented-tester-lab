# Review checklist for any AI output

The six working rules from chapter 1, as a checklist.

- [ ] **Draft, not final.** I have read every line; nothing was pasted straight into the suite.
- [ ] **Checked against the real application.** Selectors pass `npm run ch1:check-selectors`; expected results match what the app really does.
- [ ] **Detailed context was given.** The prompt included the story, criteria, constraints and expected behaviour.
- [ ] **No invention allowed.** The prompt said so, and every `ASSUMPTION` in the output is resolved or listed as an open question.
- [ ] **Recommendations questioned.** Any suggested tool, pattern or fix was checked against our team, stack and resources.
- [ ] **Decision kept with a person.** I decided what to keep, what to change and what to delete.
